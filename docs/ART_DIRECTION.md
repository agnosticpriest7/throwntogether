# Thrown Together — Art Direction

## Current direction
Final production art is not fully locked.

Current preference:
- simple stylized 3D
- 2.5D presentation
- fixed elevated camera
- very readable silhouettes
- low visual clutter
- slightly more expressive/cute characters than PlateUp
- simple geometry during prototyping

## Prototype rule
Gameplay readability and iteration speed matter more than polish.

Acceptable prototypes:
- cubes
- cylinders
- primitive appliances
- simple materials
- placeholder UI
- crude characters

Do not spend large portions of the AI budget polishing temporary assets.

## Production strategy
Once the core game is strongly proven:
1. commission a human-art visual slice
2. establish the production art bible
3. progressively replace visible prototype assets

Potential specialist work:
- characters
- animation
- appliances
- food
- environments
- UI
- key art
- music/audio

## Readability
At gameplay camera distance:
- ingredient states must be distinguishable
- appliances must read immediately
- employees/customers/players must be distinct
- active/ready/dirty/error states must be clear
- floor/environment detail must not compete with interactables
